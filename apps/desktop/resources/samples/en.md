# Small questions, deeper reading

Reading is an active conversation with an idea. Pause after a paragraph and ask: **What is the claim? What evidence supports it?**

A useful habit is to explain one idea in your own words. For example, instead of memorizing “spaced practice”, revisit a question tomorrow and again next week. The pause gives you a chance to retrieve what you remember.
